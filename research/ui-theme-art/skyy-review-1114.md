## What Skyy asks for (main session reading, checked against the frames)
1. Top bar: our coins / bank / mail / X on the right is better than Runeteria's; tabs should be compact like theirs (their buttons fit on about half the screen).
2. Later ideas (not now): leaderboards; HUD info on screen; party frames with icons + a health bar per member.
3. Pocket swap: like Runeteria's 'Back to crafting' - our row [Craft][Mining]...: picking Mining shows the Mining bag in the top panel; shift-click in / out; Deposit all / Stash to Void.
4. Character: Runeteria's utility slot = the off-hand picker - keep that idea; prefer OUR pet + Void Charms; the slot column right of the character = off-hand slots (unless we add trinkets later).
5. Crafting: prefer Runeteria's crafting system (icon category tabs on top, bench name, recipe icon grid with page arrows, 10x / MAX) over our v2 panel.
6. Skills: compact icon grid like their Professions (icon + level under), even more compact than theirs; Right now = a skinny list (unspent points ...) and the QUESTS live in Right now (their quest marker system is well done).
7. Teleports: prefer OUR teleport row at the bottom over their RTP / Homes.
8. Grids: tight and uniform slots - no big gaps.
9. Buttons: no 'slots used' line; Sort + Stash to Void move to the TOP of the inventory panel as small vanilla-style icon buttons (the normal pattern: sort, quick-stack matching items up, put all, take all); on a pocket the same buttons go to / from the Void.
10. Chests: the new inventory system must also work properly inside chests (our own chest screen).
