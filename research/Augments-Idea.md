## The idea
- Gear has augment slots (like SkyBlock gems, our own name, a few kinds).
- Two types, you choose which goes in a slot:
  - STAT augments: 2 stats that go together; weapon / armor / tool versions. Examples from Skyy: Breaking Speed + Mining Fortune,
    Strength + Crit Damage, Attack Speed + Damage, Strength + Defence, Move Speed + Mana ("any combo works").
  - ELEMENTAL augments: fuse low levels into higher ones (like powders); weapons = element damage, armor = element defence.
- Elements really work (not just a number): particles on attacks; Fire burns, Thunder staggers, Air more knockback, ...
- Elements interact realistically (Hytale's idea): Water puts out burning; Thunder and Ice stronger vs Wet; Air + Fire = hotter, longer fire;
  "i want all the elements to interact realistically."
