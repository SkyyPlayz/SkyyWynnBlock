## Feasibility + faithfulness review

1. **[medium] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md §0 ("Armor above your level ... gives no stats at all") and §3.5, cross-checked against §12 (Q1-Q17)**
   - Problem: The spec reads Skyy's short answer ('too-high armor gives no stats until the level is reached') to also cancel the piece's native Hytale health and damage-reduction — requiring a hand-copied, bytecode-derived reimplementation of the engine's armor-resistance formula (§3.5 GearArmorSys), with 5 stat types (DamageClassEnhancement, KnockbackResistances/Enhancements, DamageEnhancement, Regenerating, MovementSettings) explicitly listed as a 'known limit' that won't be cancelled. This is a much bigger, riskier engineering commitment than a plain reading of Skyy's sentence implies (it could just as easily mean 'SkyyGear's own rolled modifiers don't apply'), yet it is not listed among the spec's own Q1-Q17 open questions the way comparably interpretive scope choices (e.g. Q8, Q16) are.
   - Fix: Add an explicit open question (e.g. Q18): does 'no stats' on under-level armor also cancel the piece's native Hytale Health/Armor%, or only SkyyGear's own rolled modifiers? Keep the current (more aggressive, Wynncraft-faithful) behavior as the stated default, but surface it for Skyy's sign-off before the team builds and has to maintain a parallel copy of the engine's armor formula.
2. **[low] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md §0 ('The live stats in 0.1' bullet) vs §4.2 pool table**
   - Problem: Section 0's plain-word summary for Skyy lists live stats as '...Flat Earth, Thunder, Water, Fire and Air damage, and True Damage...' but omits Raw Thunder Damage, Raw Water Damage, and Raw Elemental damage (keys rThunder/rWater/rElem), which §4.2 explicitly marks LIVE and includes in the craft/reforge/identify roll pool. Section 0 is the section Skyy is meant to read to understand and approve scope, so this creates a real gap between what Skyy will believe ships live and what the technical spec actually implements.
   - Fix: Add the three omitted raw-element lines to §0's live-stats bullet (e.g. '...and True Damage. Also extra Raw Thunder, Raw Water and Raw Elemental damage lines.') so the plain-word summary matches §4.2 exactly.
3. **[low] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md §4.2 ('Not in the 0.1 pool, and why') vs SkyyGear-Stat-Catalog.md 'Mana and spell cost' table**
   - Problem: The catalog marks both 'Intelligence' (SB) and 'Mana' (SB) as Keep rows, but §4.2's pool table and its 'Not in the 0.1 pool, and why' exclusion list never mention them (only Max Mana, Mana Regen, Mana Steal and Health (ID) are addressed there). It's implicitly clear these two rows describe the underlying mana system rather than separate rollable stat IDs, but the spec never says so, leaving it ambiguous whether they were considered or missed.
   - Fix: Add a one-line note in §4.2 stating that the catalog's 'Intelligence' and 'Mana' rows are descriptive of Hytale's mana system, not separate rollable IDs — they're covered by Max Mana / Mana Regen / Mana Steal instead — so a future reader doesn't wonder if two Keep-tagged catalog rows were silently dropped.

## Exploit + economy review

1. **[high] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md § 5.1 GearCraftSys (lines ~470-481) and § 10 'Crafted-roll theft' row (line 898)**
   - Problem: Craft rolls are assigned by diffing inventory-section membership: snapshot stack identities at CraftRecipeEvent$Post, then in the queued CraftRollTask (runs after giveOutput, still same FIFO tick) roll any undocumented stack of that item id that is 'not in the snapshot', capped only at outputQty x quantity. Nothing ties the rolled stack to the actual crafted output object. A player who inserts other pre-existing, undocumented stacks of the same item id into their inventory in that same window (vault withdraw, trade accept, ground pickup, even a second player tossing one to them) between the snapshot and the task running gets those items rolled at full craft/Smithing odds for free — laundering old junk gear into rolled (potentially high-rarity) items without paying that craft's material cost, repeatable every craft.
   - Fix: Don't identify the 'new' stack purely by absence-from-snapshot; tag the literal stack objects returned by the specific addOrDropItemStack call that grants the craft output (mirroring how GearDropSys/GearChestTag hook the actual spawn/fill event rather than diffing). If that's not feasible with the deprecated/missing output event, at minimum block all other inventory-adding actions (trade completion, vault withdrawal, item pickup) for a player while pendingCraft[uuid] is set, the same way profile:busy already blocks item moves, so nothing else can slip into the diff window.
2. **[high] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md § 3.3 gate cache (lines ~298-311) vs § 3.6 recheck triggers (lines ~364-369)**
   - Problem: The weapon/armor level-gate result is cached per player on the world thread and refreshed only by the 1Hz GearTick or 'on profile epoch change'. A straight class change (e.g. SkyyClasses Warrior→Archer) is not listed as a cache-invalidation trigger, unlike armor's InventoryChangeEvent-driven immediate recompute. A player can grind one class's weapon skill high, switch class, and within the up-to-1s stale window land hits with a weapon above their new class's real skill level — repeatable indefinitely by rapid class-swapping since there's no stated cooldown tying a swap to the cache refresh.
   - Fix: Add the SkyyClasses class-change signal as an explicit, immediate gate-cache invalidation trigger (same treatment as profile epoch change) instead of relying on the 1Hz tick for this path.
3. **[medium] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md § 1.5 legacy stamp (lines ~136-154) vs § 5.5 'Laundering' defense (lines ~532-559) and § 10 'Laundering plain gear' row (line 896)**
   - Problem: The stamp scan that gives every held gear item a Normal record 'never runs for a player with a pending craft roll: the craft task runs first.' Because pendingCraft[uuid] can be kept effectively continuous by spamming cheap crafts, a player can hold that flag open while receiving other undocumented gear (trade, mail, ground pickup) that would otherwise get stamped almost immediately by the InventoryChangeEvent scan. The mob/chest unidentified-tagging defense in 5.5 explicitly assumes every held gear item gets a Normal record right away; extending the undocumented window lets a still-unstamped item be thrown near a dying mob and picked up by GearDropSys's tagging as a random-rarity unidentified drop instead of staying Normal.
   - Fix: Scope the 'pending craft blocks the scan' rule to only the specific item id being crafted, not the whole player, or cap how long a pending craft can suppress the stamp scan (timeout + force-stamp of everything else). Add an explicit test in § 11.4 for 'throw an unstamped item on a dying mob while spam-crafting keeps pendingCraft set'.
4. **[medium] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md § 7.1 gear:fn:sig (line 722) and § 7.3 SkyyAuctions patch item 2 (line 747)**
   - Problem: gear:fn:sig is defined as a fingerprint of 'rarity + identify state + modifiers' only — it does not include the item's base id or quality index. Two different unidentified items of the same rarity (e.g. an Unidentified Iron Sword and an Unidentified Wood Wand, both 'Rare') produce an identical sig, and SkyyAuctions' 'rolls differ' duplicate-listing caveat is keyed directly off sig equality. This lets structurally different items be treated as 'the same' by that check, undermining the one signal buyers get for comparing unidentified (or coincidentally-identical-roll) listings.
   - Fix: Fold the item id (and stack quality index) into the gear:fn:sig hash input so it cannot collide across different underlying items.
5. **[medium] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md § 6.3 tooltip refresh rules (lines ~688-695)**
   - Problem: The tooltip's sig hash includes only an owner 'flag' (the ok/not-ok boolean) and skill name, not the raw 'have' level, and re-render is stated to trigger 'when the flag flipped (level-up, profile switch)' — not on every level change. A player who levels their gate skill but stays below the requirement (e.g. 12→18 when 20 is needed) keeps seeing a stale '(you: 12)' in the tooltip indefinitely, even though the live combat check (3.6) correctly uses the current level for the actual block/allow decision. This is a real tooltip-vs-applied-stat desync, even though it isn't exploitable for gain.
   - Fix: Include the raw 'have' value in the sig hash (not just the boolean 'ok' flag), or force a re-render whenever the displayed 'have' number differs from the cached level, not only on a flag flip.
6. **[medium] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md § 1.6 migration table (lines ~156-189) and § 2.2 rarity power table (lines ~232-248)**
   - Problem: Migrated SkyyRolls items keep their old dmg/str/crit values 'even above the rarity's placeholder count or range,' but the rarity they land in comes from a standalone 0-100 'Roll Quality' score, not from those actual stat values. An old item with the maximum possible flat stats (dmg 30 / str 25 / crit 15) can still map to a middling rarity (e.g. Rare) if its stored Roll Quality happened to fall under the 75/90 thresholds, so its displayed rarity color understates its real power. Raw numbers are visible on inspection, but casual AH browsing by rarity color alone can be misled.
   - Fix: Consider deriving (or blending) the migration rarity from actual dmg/str/crit magnitude rather than the standalone Roll Quality score alone, or explicitly document/flag in the identify and AH UI that migrated items may exceed their displayed rarity's normal range.
7. **[medium] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md § 3.4 weapon gate check (lines ~313-333)**
   - Problem: The melee gate resolves the attacker's weapon via InventoryComponent.getItemInHand(buf, ref) with no mention of a second/offhand slot. If Hytale exposes any offhand-equippable item capable of dealing damage, a too-high-level weapon held there would never be checked, bypassing the level gate entirely.
   - Fix: Confirm during the engine-fact checks (§ 11.1/11.3) whether Hytale has an offhand damage-dealing slot; if it does, extend GearHitSys's weapon resolution to cover it, and add an explicit 'offhand: N/A' note to the spec if it does not.
8. **[low] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md § 1.6 migrate.map (line ~166) and § 12 Q2 (line 984)**
   - Problem: SkyyRolls keeps functioning with its own (cheaper, simpler) reforge cost/anti-farming rules right up to the same deploy that retires it, and migrated rarity is fixed from the Roll Quality score at first post-migration touch. A player aware of the cutover date has a clear incentive to spend the transition window mass-reforging under the old system purely to fish for quality ≥90 (an automatic Fabled under the default mapping), which is far easier than the new system's Fabled odds (≤1% craft, 1.5%/2.4% drop).
   - Fix: Decide explicitly whether this pre-cutover reforge-rush is acceptable; if not, tighten migrate.map thresholds, cap migrated rarity below Fabled, or disable SkyyRolls' /reforge a short window before the swap so the old system can't be farmed right up to migration.
9. **[low] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md § 5.1/5.3 crafting + Smithing odds (lines ~470-503) and § 10 'Crafting loops' row (line 897)**
   - Problem: The anti-exploit table's defense against craft/salvage reroll-farming is just 'salvage returns less (vanilla)' — an assumption, not something SkyyGear enforces — combined with no per-player/per-recipe cooldown on repeat crafts. Since crafted gear rolls (and is shown) immediately with no identify step, a cheap/renewable-material recipe can still be mass-crafted purely to fish for a Smithing-boosted high rarity, salvaging the rest back.
   - Fix: Add an explicit, SkyyGear-owned safeguard: a per-recipe/per-player craft-roll cooldown or diminishing Smithing bonus on rapid repeat crafts of the same recipe, or confirm in the spec that materials cost floor keeps expected reroll-farming value negative regardless of Smithing level, rather than relying on an unverified vanilla salvage ratio.
10. **[low] research/SkyyGear-Stage1-Spec.md § 9.2 `stat` config row (line 843)**
   - Problem: The `stat` Server Setup table (per-stat max @100% and weight) allows values up to 100000 with only structural validation (unknown ids, high<low, duplicate reforge names) in GearCfg.check*; there's no sanity ceiling tied to game balance, so a single miskeyed Server Setup edit could silently blow out every rarity's power for that stat at once.
   - Fix: Add a soft warning (not a hard block) in the config validation when a stat's max is set far outside its documented placeholder range, so an accidental typo in Server Setup is caught before it reshapes the whole economy.
