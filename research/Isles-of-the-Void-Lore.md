# Isles of the Void - story notes (IDEA, 2026-10-01; nothing renamed)

Working name for the pack / server: **Isles of the Void** (Skyy 2026-10-01; "Iles" was a typo). The code name stays SkyWynn until Skyy
decides otherwise.

## Skyy's lore (2026-10-01)

- The world is an **alternate dimension** next to Hytale's Orbis. **The Void** started fracturing worlds and pulling pieces of them in,
  along with random players.
- The Void is **everywhere and nowhere**, so it pulls things in from everywhere - the in-story reason the pack borrows ideas and elements
  from other games and servers (SkyBlock, Wynncraft, ...).
- The islands are **fragments / shards**. Bigger, older, more stable shards are the zone islands.
- **Start:** when you are pulled in you wake up on your own small **personal shard** (your SkyyIslands island) floating in the void. You
  have to figure out how to get off it. A short progression unlocks a **portal to a larger, older, more stable shard - the Zone 1 hub**
  (the temple town). From there the quest starts.
- The main goal should **not** be "get back home" (generic). Skyy wants it **silly and absurd**.

## Starter-shard progression (main-session sketch, for Skyy to shape)

1. Wake up on the shard with almost nothing (the island starter chest). A guide character talks you through it (see below).
2. Gather and craft the basics (wood, a Workbench, the Accessory Bag) - doubles as the tutorial.
3. Find the broken portal frame on the shard and repair it from shard materials (a first Collections / crafting goal).
4. Power it with a Void crystal from a tiny cave or a mini guardian on the shard (first fight, first class weapon use).
5. Step through: the Zone 1 temple, the hub, the first warp. Returning players and existing profiles skip it (one-time unlock flag).

## Main-objective pitches (silly / absurd) - pick, mix, or throw out

1. **The Void has the hiccups.** The Void swallowed something it shouldn't have (the multiverse's TV remote / a cosmic rubber duck / a whole
   moon) and every hiccup rips another chunk out of some world - that is why random stuff (and players) keep arriving, and **every content
   update is "another hiccup"**. The goal: cure the hiccups. Each zone boss guards a piece of an absurd folk remedy (scare it, make it hold its
   breath, feed it a spoon of sugar the size of an island). The finale: the Void burps up what it swallowed - the Zone 5 dragon, who was
   only ever trying to get its egg back.
2. **The Void is a lost-and-found office.** You were misplaced. To be "claimed" you must complete a ridiculous form (Proof of Existence,
   Form 27-B/6) signed by each zone boss, stamped by a sleepy Kweebec clerk who keeps losing it. Every zone adds another stamp and
   another absurd sub-form.
3. **The Void is a cosmic toddler.** A baby god scooped up bits of worlds as toys. The bosses are its toys come alive; the goal is to get it
   to finally take a nap (the fracturing stops while it sleeps) - lullabies, a giant blanket, a plush dragon.
4. **Void rent.** The Void is a landlord and you are a tenant who owes rent from the moment you arrived (your shard is a "starter
   apartment"). A goblin notary tracks your debt; the endgame is paying off a gloriously huge Void Mortgage - the coin grind with a story.

Guide character idea (any pitch): an overconfident talking rock (or a pebble-sized golem) who has "been here for 4,000 years", gives
terrible advice, and is wrong about everything - the tutorial voice on your shard and a running joke in every town.
