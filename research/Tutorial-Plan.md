# Tutorial / first hour (plan) - Skyy 2026-10-12

Skyy's words are in `docs/answered/social.md` (2026-10-12 TUTORIAL). Draft list for Skyy to trim; nothing designed in code yet. It
belongs to SkyyQuests (the quest widget) - check existing quest / tutorial mods first (PROJECT-RULES 2; QuestLines is ARR).

## Shape

- You start on your own island, pick a class, and the **quest widget** suggests what to do next (no guide fairy for now; maybe later).
- Three starter islands: **1 = home** (basics), **2 = combat**, **3 = mini boss** who drops the **Hub Shard** that activates the portal
  to the hub. You build your own way across (bridging is part of the lesson).
- **NOT linear:** every task completes the moment its condition is met, in any order, even before the widget asks. If you did task 6
  before task 4, task 6 is already ticked when you get there. The widget always shows the next 1-3 UNDONE tasks in the suggested order.
  Only real physical needs are hard requirements (the portal needs the Hub Shard).
- Per profile; each task pays a small reward once (coins / skill XP / a starter item). "I know how to play" hides the TUTORIAL tasks
  from the widget (they keep ticking quietly, rewards still paid); the widget itself stays for quests.

**Quest widget = essential** (Skyy: "we need a place to quickly and easily tell players what to do next without digging in menus"): an
always-on HUD widget with the next 1-3 tasks, progress ("Chop logs 6/10") and where to go - the tutorial lives in it, later quests too.

## Task list (suggested order)

**Island 1 - home**
1. Pick a class (class picker on first join) - shows your class weapon + first ability.
2. Open the hub buttons in the inventory (where Skills, Collections, Requip, Void, Bank live).
3. Chop 10 logs (Foraging XP, the skill list on the right).
4. Craft your first **crafting accessory** (the bench-accessory rule) and equip it.
5. Craft a pickaxe; mine stone + copper (tool tiers, tool level = skill level).
6. Open Collections: see your first rank-up and the recipe it unlocked.
7. Farm: till, plant, water, harvest (a starter plot on the island).
8. Put items into your **SkyyVoid** bag; take them out.
9. Save a **Requip** loadout (weapon + tool + armor pool).
10. Deposit coins in the **Bank** (interest).
11. Bridge to island 2 (place blocks; the widget points the way).

**Island 2 - combat**
12. Equip your class weapon and use ability 1 (Mana).
13. Defeat 5 mobs; dodge roll once; land one **perfect parry**.
14. Pick up an **unidentified** drop and identify it (Anvil / Appraisal).
15. Reforge or upgrade one item (rarity, [Lv N] names).
16. Eat food / heal up; (a short note on what death costs).
17. Hatch / equip your first **pet** (if pets are in by then).
18. Open **Unclaimed Luggage** (Lost Loot) found on the island.
19. Bridge to island 3.

**Island 3 - mini boss**
20. Defeat the mini boss -> **Hub Shard**.
21. Place the shard in the portal -> travel to the **hub**.

**Hub (first visit)**
22. Visit the Bazaar (buy or sell one thing), the Auction House, the Bank NPC, the quest board.
23. Party / guild intro (open the party menu; invite a friend - optional).
24. Your first **shard** (Mining / Foraging / Farming ...) - the automation minigame starts (research/Shard-Islands-Ideas.md).

**Starter islands (Skyy 2026-10-12):** one fixed hand-built design for everyone, but NOTHING protected - it guides, never restricts. Each
island ~4-5x today's starter island: bushes, rubble, blocks to mine, old broken structures; enough to make crude tools and bridge on.
The "make crude tools" step = crafting your one lifelong starter tool set (spec to settle vs "spawn with a pickaxe and an axe").
