# Class roles sheet - stat ratings per class (SKYY-APPROVED 2026-10-18, popups; fine-tune by feel)

Why: Skyy (docs/answered/skills.md 10-18) "take the draft roles, and lest refine each one from there ... i want to fully spec their role,
weight, and where they stand on each stat. (not exact numbers but super low, low mid high and super high for each stat.)"
Scale: SL super low, L low, M mid, H high, SH super high. Roles come from research/classes/<Class>.md.
WEIGHT sets base move speed; lighter gear = faster, heavier = slower, and a heavy class is slowed much less by heavy gear than a light
class (Skyy 10-18). Skyy edits this table (or answers the popups); the balance spec (research/Balance-Pass-Spec.md) turns it into numbers.
Where answers go: this file (mark a row DONE) + docs/answered/classes.md. Index: INDEX.md.

| Class | Main / second role | Weight | Health | Defense | Mana | Stamina | Speed | Damage | Range | Healing / support | Crowd control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Tank / crowd control | SH | SH | SH | L | H | L | M | SL | M (party shield) | H |
| Berserker | Party damage buffer / sustained melee | H | H | M | L | SH | M | H | SL | H (damage buffs) | M |
| Spellblade | Frontline area damage / boss debuffs | M | M | M | H | M | M | H | L | L | H |
| Monk | Disruptor (speed + combos) / single-target control | L | M | L | M | SH | SH | M | L | L | M |
| Assassin | Priority killer (burst) / debuffer | SL | L | L | M | H | SH | SH (single target) | M (kunai; a bit better close up) | SL | L |
| Archer | Crowd control / focus marker, ranged | L | M | M | M | H | H | H | SH | L | SH |
| Mage | Burst damage / survival | SL | SL | SL | SH | L | H | SH | H | L | M |
| Priest | Healer / protector | L | M | M | SH | L | H | L | SH (more than Mage, pierce / AoE) | SH | L |

Rule from Skyy (Mage answer): speed follows weight - a super-light class has at least High speed.

Notes:
- Every class can solo (Skyy 10-18); support classes (Priest, Berserker) are slower solo but much stronger in groups.
- Each class has 2-3 H/SH strengths and 2-3 L/SL weaknesses, so no class is good at everything.
- Casters run dry every 20-30 s of non-stop casting (Mage, Priest, Spellblade); Stamina classes (Monk, Berserker, Assassin) lean on
  dodges / sprints instead.
