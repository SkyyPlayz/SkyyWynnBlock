# Story script - Zones 2 to 5 (the "where are you really from" chain)

Cloud draft, 2026-10-03. Writing only; no quest system exists yet. Continues `research/cloud/Story-Script-Draft.md` (cast, tone, Zone 1 chain).
Locks used (OPEN-QUESTIONS Q&A 2026-10-02): guardians at 20 / 30 / 45 / 60; **Zone 5 = the dinosaur caves under Zone 4, Lv 60-75, dragon boss at 75**;
Zone 2 **stable quest** unlocks the Summon slot and the first mount pet; **one dragon per profile**; outposts share biome variants; each zone town is built around a
vanilla temple with a warp. Names are working names. Same tone: silly, short lines, numbers always slightly wrong.

## 1. Zone 2 - which VERSION (Howling Sands, Lv 20-30)

### 1.1 Quests

| # | Quest | The player does | Checks | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Z2.1 | **Page Two Of Three** | Arrive; Registrar Dune reads Stamp 1 and sighs: "Hytale. Yes. But WHICH Hytale?" | arrival flag | Annex map; warp unlocked |
| Z2.2 | **Early Access Anxiety** | Collect three "version fragments" from outposts: a faded patch-notes scroll (Golden Steppes), a bug report in a bottle (Oasis), a sticky note "TODO: fix everything" (Desolate Basin ruin). | 3 pickups | Version Ledger (stage 1) |
| Z2.3 | **Diff Of The Dunes** | Match your gear and items against the ledger: bring one item from three different "eras" (a Crude item, a Copper item, a Cobalt item). Registrar Dune compares them by "feel". | item check | clue |
| Z2.4 | **The Stable Quest (Summon slot)** | The stable master in the town: rescue a runaway camel from the Scarak edge, bring it carrots from the Golden Steppes. | camel returned | **Summon slot unlocked + first mount pet (Camel)** - LOCKED by Skyy |
| Z2.5 | **Release Candidate** | Kill the Scarak Broodmother's champions in the nest, then the Broodmother (guardian Lv 30) for page 3. | guardian kill | **Stamp 2: VERSION - "Early Access 0.6.8 (or the pre-release)"**, Portal Fragment to Zone 3 |

### 1.2 Dialogue

**Arrival**
> **Registrar Dune** *(fanning himself with a form)*: Stamp one says Hytale. Lovely. Which Hytale? There was an early access, a pre-release, a "we'll see", and something that is still loading.
> *(Player: How would I know?)*
> **Registrar Dune:** Quite right. You wouldn't. That's why I exist. Bring me version fragments. Anything with a number and a bug.
> *HIC. A cactus changes colour.*
> **Registrar Dune:** Don't worry, that's just the version updating.

**Z2.2 fragments**
> *(Patch-notes scroll)*: "Fixed: sand. Known issue: more sand."
> *(Bug report in a bottle)*: "Steps to reproduce: be here. Expected: not here. Actual: here."
> *(Sticky note)*: "TODO: everything. DONE: nothing. BLOCKED BY: the sun."

**Z2.3 Diff**
> **Registrar Dune** *(holding up your Copper item, then your Cobalt item)*: This one is older. This one is newer. This one is... a rock. Is this a rock?
> **Pebble** *(appearing)*: Excuse me, I'm ticket #2.
> **Registrar Dune:** Not now, Pebble.
> **Pebble:** I was there for the pre-release. I remember it fondly. It was a rock.

**Z2.4 Stable master (name: Stablehand Hemi, a Feran)**
> **Hemi:** My camel Sheila has run off. She does this when she feels "unrecorded". Bring her back with carrots. And be nice - she's an emotional animal.
> *(Camel found at the nest edge.)*
> **Sheila (the camel):** *(spits, then leans on you affectionately)*
> **Hemi:** She likes you. That's rare. Take her. Don't tell her she's a pet, she thinks she's your supervisor. *(unlocks the Summon slot)*

**Z2.5 Broodmother**
> **Broodmother:** I am the keeper of page three. Page two was simply a draft.
> *(fight)*
> **Broodmother (defeated):** Fine. Take page three. Mind the eggs. They are still a draft.

## 2. Zone 3 - which WORLD / SAVE (Whisperfrost Frontiers, Lv 30-45)

### 2.1 Quests

| # | Quest | The player does | Checks | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Z3.1 | **Cold Storage** | Arrive at the frozen archive; Archivist Frostwick checks Stamps 1 and 2: "Hytale, early access. Good. Now WHICH WORLD?" | arrival flag | warp unlocked |
| Z3.2 | **Lost Saves** | Find three "save files" (stone slabs in the Everfrost ruins, ice-buried): each one is a world you might be from - a Plains one, a Frozen one, a Void one. | 3 pickups | Save Index (stage 1) |
| Z3.3 | **Defrost The Records** | Light the three braziers in the archive so the paperwork can thaw - each brazier needs fuel from a different biome (Redwood log, frozen berry, tundra coal). | 3 fuels delivered | clue |
| Z3.4 | **Which Seed?** | Frostwick reads your item "feel" and asks you to name your world seed. Pick a number from options (all wrong, all accepted). | choice flag | clue |
| Z3.5 | **The Cold Case** | The archive's door is frozen shut by the Yeti, who wants a warm blanket. Beat the Yeti (guardian Lv 45) for page 4. | guardian kill | **Stamp 3: WORLD - "a world with a Forgotten Temple. Probably the one with the bears."**, Portal Fragment to Zone 4 |

### 2.2 Dialogue

**Arrival**
> **Archivist Frostwick** *(wearing seven scarves)*: Welcome to Cold Storage. Everything is filed. I can't remember where. That is the beauty of the system.
> *(Player: I need to know which world I'm from.)*
> **Archivist Frostwick:** Naturally. Which world? Worlds come with seeds, saves, and sometimes a very opinionated cat.
> *(Player: I don't know.)*
> **Archivist Frostwick:** That's the most common answer. We've made a form for it.

**Z3.2 Lost Saves**
> *(Slab 1)*: "Save 04: a flat world. Very tidy. Zero hills."
> *(Slab 2)*: "Save 117: one tall tree. Someone loved it."
> *(Slab 3)*: "Save 2: the Void. Entries: 4,000,000,001 (including yours)."

**Z3.4 Which Seed?**
> **Archivist Frostwick:** What is your world seed?
> *(options: 7 / 7,000 / "Whichever one has the bears")*
> **Archivist Frostwick:** Excellent. That matches nothing, so it must be right.

**Z3.5 The Yeti**
> **Yeti:** The archive is closed. Closed means I lean on it.
> *(fight)*
> **Yeti (at 50%):** I only wanted a blanket!
> **Yeti (defeated):** ...I'll hold the door. Take page four. It is *very* cold. Is that a blanket?

**Z4.4 Almost Right**
> *(machine hums; HIC)*

> *(Result screen)*: "Your coordinates are: x = 4,000,000,001, y = 2, z = 'ask the dragon'."
> **Dr. Voidwright:** The z-axis is the hard one. The z-axis requires a dragon. I'm not saying it's a good plan. I'm saying it's the plan.

**Z4.5 Ember Warden**
> **Ember Warden:** I'm a furnace with ambitions.
> *(fight)*
> **Ember Warden (defeated):** I... didn't read page five either. Take it. It says "SEE THE DRAGON."
> **Dr. Voidwright:** *(running in)* Don't touch the lava. Or do. Science!

## 4. Zone 5 - the Dragon (the dinosaur caves, Lv 60-75)

### 4.1 Zone setup
Zone 5 is the cave land under the Zone 4 caldera (OPEN-QUESTIONS R8): raptors, rexes, pterodactyls; Lv 60-75; the Egg Desk camp is the town; the dragon boss is Lv 75 and needs gear above 49 (our own tiers) - plan the quests so the player reaches the Egg Desk at Lv 60 and the dragon fight at 75.

### 4.2 Quests

| # | Quest | The player does | Checks | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Z5.1 | **The Egg Desk** | Arrive at the camp; the Camp Clerk (a bone-whistling Slothian-like NPC, UNVERIFIED model) checks Stamps 1-4 and gets very excited. | arrival flag | warp unlocked |
| Z5.2 | **Dino Diplomacy** | Gather three offerings from the caves for the dragon's nest (a raptor feather, a rex tooth, a pterodactyl egg - hunt the three elite dinosaurs). | 3 drops | Nest offerings |
| Z5.3 | **Where Does A Dragon Sleep?** | Follow the dragon's tracks through the caves to the nest; avoid the packs. | position flag | clue |
| Z5.4 | **The Borrowed Egg** | The dragon waits. Dialogue + fight (the dragon only wants its egg back; you fight to prove you're worthy, not to kill). | boss fight | **The Last Stamp** + **dragon egg** (LOCKED: one dragon per profile) |
| Z5.5 | **Taking The Long Way Home** | The dragon lands: it can find your dimension. Choose: hatch the egg (Dragon quest line) or ask for a ride home. | choice flag | the ending branch below |

### 4.3 Dialogue

**Arrival**
> **Camp Clerk:** Stamps! Four stamps! In three hundred years nobody has brought four stamps. Sit. You'll want the dragon. Everyone wants the dragon. The dragon doesn't want you. Yet.

**Z5.4 The Dragon**
> **Dragon** *(landing, ground shakes)*: You are the one with the form.
> *(Player: I have four stamps.)*
> **Dragon:** I have an egg. It has been missing. The Void took it. Hic. I have been trying to get it back. Hic. Every hiccup is me hiccupping.
> *(Player: The hiccups are YOU?)*
> **Dragon:** Embarrassing. Prove you can be trusted with it. Then I will show you home.
> *(fight; the dragon never uses lethal-final moves; at 25% it drops to a knee)*
> **Dragon:** Enough. You are worthy. The egg is yours to look after. ...and it is your last stamp. *(stamp)*

**Z5.5 Home (the choice)**
> **Dragon:** I can fly you to your dimension. Hytale. The one with the bears. Or you can stay and raise the egg.
> *(Player: What happens if I go?)*
> **Dragon:** You go home. Then... hic. We probably end up here again.
> **Dragon** *(updated 2026-10-06, if "Go home" is chosen)*: Home. Lovely. Hic. But first: paperwork. Your shard has a balance. Ask the clerk in the waiting room - prestige needs a Receipt. *(opens `tab.q1_fees`; Prestige-Spec.md section 1, Tab-Prestige-Scripts.md 3.1)*
> *(Choice A: "Stay and raise the egg" -> the dragon quest line starts (Dragon-Quest-Spec).)*
> *(Choice B: "Go home" -> (updated 2026-10-06: the dragon first sends you to the clerk; prestige needs **Paid in Full** and runs through the clerk's Prestige page, not straight from the dragon - Prestige-Spec.md section 1, Tab-Prestige-Scripts.md section 3.1 and Q1) the clerk's prestige reset: permanent perks; a hiccup pulls you straight back: "Welcome back! Your ticket number is #4,000,000,002." - Skyy's favourite ending, kept exactly.)*

**The Tab hand-off:** the dragon mentions in passing that "your shard has accrued some fees". That opens the Tab (rent) quest line - see the Tab-Economy task.
