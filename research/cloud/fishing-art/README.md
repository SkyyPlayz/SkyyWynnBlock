# Fishing gear - concept icons (rods, reels, hooks, lines, sinkers)

Cloud draft, 2026-10-06. Paper design; nothing built. Original pixel art, generated by code
(`research/cloud/fishing-art/make_fishing.py`, Python + Pillow, deterministic); no copied art, no game files.
Inputs read: `research/cloud/SkyyFishing-Spec-Draft.md` section 4 (rod / reel tiers T0-T7, part tiers I-IV, the 12 part variants and
their materials), `research/cloud/light-armor/make_sheets.py` (metal ramps), `docs/answered/gear.md` lines 73-78 (art review: icons
64 x 64 like vanilla, 2x-4x more detail than v1), `docs/answered/skills.md` (FISHING lock 2026-10-06: line to the bobber, our own rods).

## 2. Rods and reels (8 tiers)

| T | Metal | Rod look | Reel look |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Bamboo | segmented bamboo blank with nodes, twine grip, wooden seat and cap | wooden reel, twine-bound rim, wooden crank knob |
| 1 | Copper | copper blank, cork grip, copper wraps / seat / cap | copper frame, 3 spokes, rim rivets |
| 2 | Iron | iron blank, cork grip | iron frame |
| 3 | Thorium | chunky double collar rings at the seat (Thorium's heavy shapes) | thick extra rim |
| 4 | Cobalt | angular fin on the seat | two angular fins on the frame |
| 5 | Adamantite | crystal shards on the butt cap | 4 spikes on the rim |
| 6 | Mithril | small wing on the seat (vanilla Mithril wings echo), black leather grip | wing on the frame, leather knob |
| 7 | Onyxium | gold wraps, gold-ringed cap with a glowing pink gem | gold inner ring, glowing hub, onyx knob |

Every rod shows 4 standing guides, a tip top and the **line to a red / white bobber** (the spec's "line + bobber" look). Every reel
shows the wound line as rings on the spool, rim rivets, a crank arm and a loose line end. Metal ramps are copied from the light-armor
`TIERS` list, so a rod matches the same tier's armor.

## 3. Parts (part tier I-IV = rod T0-T1 / T2-T3 / T4-T5 / T6-T7)

Material per tier follows the spec ("hooks = metal bars, lines = fibres, sinkers = stone"); pips in the corner = part tier.

| Part | Variants (one row each) | Tier material I / II / III / IV |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | **Barbed** (3 extra barbs on the shank), **Lost Property** (luggage tag with a red "LOST" stamp tied to the eye), **Lure** (spoon lure with a red eye + red / white feather skirt), **Monster** (stage 3: big treble hook with bound fangs) | Copper / Iron / Cobalt / Mithril (UNVERIFIED pick: the spec says "bars of the tier's metal" without naming one per part tier) |
| Line (on a spool) | **Braided** (braid pattern), **Steady** (glossy slime / sap coat with drips), **Twin** (two spools, two colours, one shared strand), **Scholar's** (paper label with writing + a quill) | Plant Fibre (green) / Linen / Silk / Cindercloth (ember orange); spool flanges in the hook metal |
| Sinker | **Weighted** (pear-shaped stone, metal collar), **Clean** (polished round stone, bubbles), **Deep** (long bell weight, down-arrow inlay, purple enchant glint), **Ember** (stage 3, one item: basalt with glowing cracks, gold ring) | Cobblestone / Sandstone (layered) / Slate (cleaved) / Basalt |

## 4. Style notes

64 x 64 grid like vanilla item icons; 1-px dark outline; 5-step ramps lit from the top-left; texture noise on metal, wood, cork and
stone (grain lines, speckle, pits, glints); transparent icon background (the sheet tiles are grey only for viewing).
