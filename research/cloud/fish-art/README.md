## 2. How a fish is drawn

- Each fish is a parameter set (length, depth above / below, peduncle, head bluntness, tail type fork / lunate / round / truncate /
  heterocercal / eel point, dorsal / anal / pelvic / pectoral fins, eye, gill, snout extension, barbels, eel wave), drawn by a small
  "fish-space" renderer: every pixel is mapped into the rotated fish frame, so the 1-px outline stays clean at any angle.
- Side view, head up-right (8-34 degrees; long fish steeper so they fill the slot), lit top-left. Shading: back colour on top with a
  highlight band, belly colour below a dithered split, darker belly rim and tail root, 4-6 steps per ramp, hash texture noise.
- Detail per icon: overlapping scale arcs with light edges (smooth skin on catfish, eels, sturgeons, lava fish), fin rays, gill arc with a
  lit rim behind it, eye with dark rim, two-tone iris, pupil and white glint, mouth line, dotted lateral line, pectoral fin over the body.
- Every fish is auto-fitted as large as possible inside a 5..58 px box, so the halo and sparkles never touch the edge.

## 4. Rarity on the icon (same idea as `accessory-art`)

| Legendary | 3-px soft halo + 3 sparkles | `#55FFFF` |
| Fabled | pale inner rim + red 3-px halo + 4 sparkles + 4 ember motes | `#FF5555` |
| Mythic | pale pink inner rim + purple 3-px halo + 5 sparkles + 6 orbiting motes | `#CC66CC` (page purple) |

Junk has no mark (grey label). Lost Property reuses the marks by treasure tier: Good = none, Great = Rare mark, Outstanding = Legendary
mark (question 2).

## 6. v2 touch-up (2026-10-06)

Only 11 icons changed; the other 43 PNGs are byte-identical to v1 (checked with md5). Two runs still give the same bytes.

| Icon(s) | v1 problem | v2 change |
|---|---|---|
| Mirebridge Eel, Mirage Eel, Permit Lamprey, Ashen Eel, Drake-Eel, Final Notice Leviathan | thin S-curves, about half the pixels of a round fish | new curled-spine renderer (`curl` heading knots; every pixel is projected onto the bent centre line, so the outline stays clean); bodies about 60% thicker. Each eel has its own pose: Mirebridge S, Mirage hook (fade kept), Lamprey S-hook, Ashen C, Drake-Eel open coil, Leviathan full coil with bigger horns. Now 1100-1800 opaque px (round fish 900-1250) in a 51-54 px box, so about 80% of the slot each way |
| Blizzard Halibut | round blob | flat oval body, a fringe fin running all the way round top and bottom (rays + frosted tips), both eyes stacked on the top side of the head, a pale blind-side rim along the lower edge (seen a little from above), a lateral line that arches high over the pectoral, a small round tail, darker slate top so the frost spots show |
| Mirage / Whisper / Bone Pit Sturgeon | one shared body | each has its own shape. **Mirage** (Z2): slim, long pointed snout, a long thread trailing from the upper tail lobe, a row of gold studs and gold spikes along the back, a lavender mirage stripe, 2 amber barbels. **Whisper** (Z3): deep, heavy beluga-like body, short upturned snout, two rows of crisp white diamond scutes on a dark blue back, 4 long barbels. **Bone Pit** (Z5): stout and armoured, upturned pointed snout, big bone plates with dark rims (one back row, one flank row) and bone spikes along the back, purple skin with bio dots |
| Slag Ray | the only top-down icon; read as a square kite | kept top-down (a ray in side view is just a line) but redrawn: swept pointed wings, rounded snout, pelvic lobes, a whip tail with two finlets and a sting, turned head-up (68 degrees) so the diamond reads as a ray and not a square. Plain obsidian head shield with big amber eyes and spiracles, pale spine ridge, cracks only on the wings, glowing wing rims |

Code (all in `make_fish.py`): `spine` / `curl_maps` / `curl_frame` / `curl_fit` (curled eels), `draw_eye` (eye drawing split out so the halibut can have two), `jaw_w` / `jaw_p` / `jaw_c` / `jaw_up` (snout shape), `barb_u` / `barb_v` / `barb_n` / `barb_s` / `barb_r` (barbels), `fil` (tail thread), `x_plates` (sprite scutes) and `x_spikes`. With these left at their defaults, every other fish draws exactly as in v1.

Still open:

- Sturgeon barbels and the Mirage thread are 1 px wide and hard to see at 1x.
- Small text on paper items (Form 27-B, ticket) is scribble, not readable words, on purpose at 64 px.
