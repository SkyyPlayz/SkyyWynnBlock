## 2. What it is

1. **Cloth armor** = Mage + Priest: Health + Mana, the most movement speed, least Defense, Mana Regen on higher tiers (locks
   2026-10-05). Ladder: **Crude Robe (this, Lv 1-13)** -> vanilla Wool -> Linen -> Cotton -> Silk -> Cindercloth tunics.
2. Recipe idea (armor-types draft, Q14 there): `Ingredient_Fibre` + `Ingredient_Crystal_Green` (Earth Crystal Golem drop). So the look is
   **crude**: undyed / roughly dyed plant fibre, a rough weave, a rope belt, and a few raw green crystal shards used as the clasp and a
   medallion - no metal at all.
3. It is the starter version of Skyy's green hooded mage robe: hooded, layered cloak, rune trim, medallions - kept simple and home-made.

## 3. The look, part by part

| Part | Design | Piece (4-piece set) |
|---|---|---|
| Hood | green hood up, deep opening with the face in shadow (dark lining under the brow fading onto the face), stitched undyed-fibre rim; back: plain hood with a seam | Hood |
| Clasp | a **big raw green crystal shard** tied with a rope cord at the throat (one glow pixel) | Hood |
| Mantle | short layered cape over the shoulders in darker moss green, **ragged hem** (cut notches), fibre trim; a 2nd green layer under the hood | Robe |
| Cloak | long outer cloak: open at the front (two panels framing the robe, each with a **stitched rune strip**), closed on the back with fold lines and a **rune-band hem** | Robe |
| Robe | long green robe flaring to the ankles, rough weave (uneven slubs), a fibre **rune band** above the ragged hem | Robe / Trousers |
| Sleeves | wide bell sleeves with rough fibre cuffs, bare hands | Robe (Wraps = cuffs?) |
| Belt | twisted **rope belt**, knot on the wearer's right with two long frayed rope ends; on the back a plain knot | Robe |
| Medallion | a small crystal shard in a fibre-wrapped ring, hung from the belt on two cords | Robe |
| Feet | fibre-wrapped shoes peeking under the hem | Wraps |

Runes are small 3 x 3 stitched glyphs (dark thread on fibre) - original shapes, not any real alphabet. Crystal = 76 of 2744 armor pixels
on the front (2.8%): "a few shards", the robe stays cloth.

## 5. Notes

- Later cloth tiers are vanilla tunics (Skyy: "nvm! hytale has tunics"); the full green mage-robe look stays "an idea for later". This
  sheet only draws tier 1, and its crude look leaves room for a fancier robe line later (metal medallions, glowing runes).
- Shapes that likely need new 3D geometry: the hood (up), the long skirt below the knees, bell sleeves, the mantle and the hanging rope
  ends. Texture-only fallback: paint the weave, trims, runes and shards on the vanilla tunic model.
