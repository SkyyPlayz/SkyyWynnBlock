## 2. What it is

1. **Heavy armor** = the Plate type (Warrior + Berserker): slower, most Defense + Health, + Crit Damage for the matching class (locks
   2026-10-05). Its ladder: **Heavy Leather (this, Lv 1-13)** -> vanilla Copper ... Onyxium metal armor as it looks today.
2. Vanilla already has `Armor_Leather_Heavy` (Skyy's screenshot of the Heavy Leather Cuirass). We do not have its look in the cloud, so
   this is **our own restyle in words -> pixels**: a thick brown / tan padded leather set that must read **heavier and bulkier than
   Light** at a glance.
3. Same mannequin, grid (64 x 84), outline and 4-step top-left shading as the Light sheets, so the two types sit side by side.

## 3. The look, part by part

| Part | Design | Why it reads "heavy" |
|---|---|---|
| Helm | padded leather cap, tan padded brow band with 4 iron studs, cheek guards, nose guard; back: layered neck guard | covers most of the head (Light has no helm) |
| Collar | thick tan padded roll (gorget) with a dark strap | bulk around the neck |
| Pauldrons | 3 layered rounded plates per shoulder, much wider than Light (x 6-23 vs 10-23), tan padding showing under each edge, rows of iron studs, strap tab on top | the biggest silhouette change |
| Cuirass | boiled-leather breastplate with a centre ridge, two riveted chest plates, 3 overlapping abdomen bands with studs, dark shoulder straps with brass rivets; tan quilted gambeson showing at the sides | layered plates instead of quilted scales |
| Back | the same plate with 3 bands, crossed dark straps meeting at a brass-studded boss | |
| Belt | thick double belt (7 rows), big 7 x 7 brass buckle, brass studs, studded pouch on the left hip | thicker than Light's belt |
| Hips | padded tan skirt under 3 studded leather tassets (front), 2 on the back | wide plates instead of thin tassels |
| Arms | tan quilted sleeves, thick banded bracers with studs, full leather gauntlets (no bare fingers) | |
| Legs | quilted tan trousers, big round knee cops with a raised boss, studded banded greaves, heavy dark boots with a tan cuff roll and brass buckles | |

Bulk check (printed by the script): front view = **2852 armor pixels vs 2178 for Light Copper (+31%)**.

## 5. Notes

- Heavy tier 1 is drawn **brown / tan**, Light is **black leather**: the two leather families never look alike.
- The sheet puts the Light Copper figure first so the size difference is visible.
- Shapes that likely need new 3D geometry (if the vanilla heavy leather model has none): the wide layered pauldrons, the tassets, the
  knee bosses and the cheek / nose guards. Texture-only fallback: paint the bands, studs and padding on the vanilla model as is.

## 6. v2 detail pass (2026-10-06)

Skyy approved the design ("Both good", `docs/answered/gear.md` 2026-10-06 art review) and asked for 2x-4x detail on every sheet.
v2 = the **same design**, redrawn at 2x; the v1 files above stay unchanged for comparison.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `make_heavy_v2.py` | v2 generator (Python + Pillow, deterministic). Imports the v2 painter of `../light-armor/make_sheets_v2.py` (unchanged). |
| `heavy-armor-sheet-v2.png` | Overview: Light Copper v2 for scale, Heavy front, back, cuirass close-up (5x) + palette |
| `heavy-leather-set-v2.png` | Front + back at 4x (512 x 768 per figure) |
| `heavy-leather-chest-v2.png` | Cuirass + pauldrons close-up at 4x |

What changed (detail only - every part keeps its v1 place, v1 (x, y) -> v2 (2x, 2y + 22)):

- Figure grid **128 x 192** (v1 64 x 84), 7-step ramps (v1 5) from the same v1 colours, whole-part gradient lit top-left, 2-px bevel.
- **Boiled leather:** mottled hide, pores, wax flecks, scuffs; a pressed (tooled) groove inside the chest plates, cuirass and top
  pauldron caps; light saddle stitching (dashes) along plate edges and under every band seam.
- **Overlapping bands:** shadow above each seam, dark seam, lit top lip of the band below (greaves, tassets, abdomen, bracers, neck guard).
- **Padding:** puffed quilted channels with stitched seams and a linen weave (gambeson, sleeves, trousers); rolled padding on the
  collar, boot cuffs, tasset and pauldron rims.
- **Studs:** 4 x 4 domed iron studs with a highlight and a cast shadow; brass studs on the belt; small rivets on the strap tabs.
- **Brass:** bevelled 16 x 16 belt buckle with prong and the strap passing through; boot buckles with prongs; belt holes.
- Gauntlets get finger lines + knuckle pads, boots a hobnailed sole and a toe cap, the helm stitched panel seams + a top knob, the
  back bracers a lacing.
- Sizes: sheet 42 KB, set 19 KB, close-up 7 KB.
