# SkyyDungeons — plan
*Design lock 2026-09-23 night. See `SkyWynn-Master-Plan.md` Part 2B steps 7–9 and 2E (P6–P7), and `SkyWynn-Decisions.md` rows 6.7, 7.4, 7.5, 7.6.*

## The spine

Dungeons on the way up are **story beats** woven through the **island chain** (`SkyyIslands-Plan.md`).

- The chain is one floating island per Hytale zone, with biomes that ramp difficulty as you cross it.
- A zone's story-beat dungeon is part of crossing that island, not a separate leveling world.
- Finish the zone's island before the next unlocks. The beat sits on that path.

After the **last** island there is **one** endgame **capstone** dungeon. That is the only dungeon that closes the spine. Row 7.5 (the floor-style / Catacombs-like dungeon) is this capstone, not a second climb.

## Slayers

**Core loop**, not side content (batch 2, row 6.7). Players meet them while they play, the way parties and guilds are part of the loop.

They are not the leveling spine. The spine stays the island chain, its story beats, and the one capstone. A slayer does not unlock the next zone island.

## Side content

These stay in the game and stay **off the spine**. They do not unlock the next zone island:

- Extra dungeons and corrupted hard modes (row 7.4, beyond the story beats)
- Raids (row 7.6)
- Lootruns, world events, and guild territory, which were already later-phase side systems

Dungeoneering as its own skill stays later (row 2.14). It is not required to walk the chain.

## Phase

- P4: slayers start with combat. They stay core through the rest of the climb.
- P6: hub remains spawn / social; the island chain ships with its story-beat dungeons.
- P7: the capstone, then the side content above. Slayers are not in that pile.

Encounter Manager is still the intended tooling when it is in the build we ship (decision 9.6, master plan Part 4). This plan does not name dungeons, floors, or bosses.
