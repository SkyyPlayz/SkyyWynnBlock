# What to watch for (paste this to Grok with the video)

You are reviewing a gameplay recording of **SkyWynn**, a Hytale modpack (RPG classes, skills, gear rarities, sky islands). The player
is testing new features. Besides anything the player says out loud or in their message, report the things below.

**Answer format:** a list of `mm:ss - what happened - why it looks wrong (or "OK")`, grouped by the sections below. Quote any on-screen
text exactly (popups, chat, tooltips, item names, numbers). Say what the player was doing just before. Mark how sure you are
(sure / likely / unsure). At the end: the 5 most important problems, worst first.

## Bugs and glitches
- Anything that happens twice when it should happen once (double launch, double sound, two popups, an item given twice).
- Something that should happen but doesn't (a button press with no effect, an ability with no animation, no sound, no damage number).
- Items disappearing, duplicating, or landing in the wrong slot; inventory or menu showing stale numbers after an action.
- Pages / menus that open blank, flicker, overlap, cut off text, or don't close; error text or red messages anywhere.
- Rubber-banding, stutters, freezes, falling through blocks, getting stuck, the camera snapping.

## Feel and balance
- Moves (dodge roll, wand launch, Pole-Vault, double jump, parries): too far / too short / too fast / too floaty, delays before or after.
- Fights: enemies dying too fast or too slow for the gear shown; damage numbers that look too big or too small; Mana or stamina
  running out too fast or never.
- Gathering: how many hits a block takes; whether a tool feels too slow for its level.
- Anything the player has to repeat many times or waits on (grind or friction worth smoothing).

## UI and look
- Text too small, unreadable colours, misaligned boxes, inconsistent fonts or styles between menus, overlapping HUD elements.
- Anything that looks like a "mod" rather than part of the game (Skyy wants it to feel like its own game: consistent, smooth,
  deliberate, a magical flowing style).
- Confusing moments: the player hesitates, opens the wrong menu, hovers around looking for something.

## Sound
- Missing, doubled, late, too loud or wrong sounds (e.g. a hit with no hit sound, a level-up with no chime).

## World
- Empty or repetitive areas, floating or cut-off terrain, structures half-generated, mobs stuck or spawning inside blocks.
