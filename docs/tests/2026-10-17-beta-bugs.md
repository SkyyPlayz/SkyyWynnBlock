## Design notes from Skyy (not bugs, need a decision or spec before building)

Skyy's ideas from the voice track. Each one needs a spec or a "yes" from Skyy first; ideas go into `research/*.md`.

- **Pets:** stats too low. Common Lv 50 = 25 Farming Fortune; combat pet Lv 50 = 25 Strength, 25 Crit Damage, 10 Crit Chance;
  scale up with rarity; better pets boost that skill's XP. Pet level buffs feel meaningless now.
- **Hub:** only admins can skip the portal; players must beat the boss and open the portal.
- **Vault:** vault and bank are the same - drop one. Put Vault between Market and Merchants. The shortcut opens straight to the
  chest; ideally one scrolling chest that grows with upgrades (only if safe). Withdraw to the hotbar unless it is full.
- **Watering can:** add a water source on the island.
- **Quests:** show progress counts (how much stone, copper ...); a quest-complete banner for the Hub Shard.
- **Furnace / benches:** click a fuel or metal slot to pick from Void storage.
- **Food:** say which buffs it gives; regen over time, not instant.
- **Combat:** wider parry window (a bit early still parries, but not hold-block); no slow while blocking; parry counter hits
  harder; slower regen on the island boss.
- **Identify:** keep rolls within about 4 levels of the player.
- **Skills:** Farming too slow, Foraging too fast, Zen levels faster than Spellblade. Classes at the top of the skills list.
  Turn on all trees + a skill-tree pass. Side menu: Farming, Orchard, Ranching separate, no overall Farming level.
- **Skip:** less stamina regen and no Mana regen while skipping ("a lot of skip ... but not infinite"). The jump-off-and-crouch
  trick is allowed - "do not block it."
- **Crafting / Market:** craftable recipes sort to the top; upgrades at the top, not-yet-crafted at the bottom. Raise buy prices a
  bit. Buy 1 / 10 / 100 / custom, or buy-all with a confirm. Red / green have-it display everywhere (pets, eggs, Orchard).
- **Loot chests:** per player and per profile.
- **Fishing:** spawn by the island pond; fish per zone with tiers; pole and reel follow the metal tiers (fits the fishing rebuild,
  on hold).
- **Working well (keep):** Void Charms + Food Pouch, the apple fix, /sethome.
